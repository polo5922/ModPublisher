## ✨ At a glance

- 💎 **12 crystal blocks** that light up at night
- 🌍 **4 underground biomes**: find them with `/locate biome crystalcaves:<biome>`

| Crystal | Light |
|---------|-------|
| Azure   | 10    |
| Violet  | 12    |


## Installation

1. Install **Fabric API**.
2. Put the jar in your `mods` folder.

*Have fun! 💎*
